## Already riding?

- **[Pair with your training app]({% link getting-started/first-ride.md %}#pair-smartspin2k-to-your-training-app).** Pick SmartSpin2k for power, cadence, and the controllable trainer.
- **[Change your settings]({% link documentation/configuration.md %}).** Shifter direction, shift step, ERG sensitivity, and more.
- **[Calibrate]({% link getting-started/calibration.md %}).** Teach SmartSpin2k the range of your bike's knob. Optional.
- **[Add a heart rate monitor]({% link documentation/bluetooth.md %}).** SmartSpin2k passes it to your training app with everything else.
- **[Update the firmware]({% link documentation/firmware.md %}).** Get the latest fixes and features from the Companion App.
