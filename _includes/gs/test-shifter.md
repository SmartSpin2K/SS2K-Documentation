1. On **Virtual Shifter**, tap **Shift up**. The resistance knob turns and **VIRTUAL GEAR** goes up by one.
2. Tap **Shift down**. The knob turns back.
3. Press the up button, then the down button, on the shifter on your handlebar. The gear number follows.

### Shifter reversed?

If the up button on your shifter lowers the gear, swap the direction.

1. On **Device**, tap **Settings**, then **Basic**, then **Swap Shifter Direction**.
2. Flip the switch, then tap **SAVE**.
