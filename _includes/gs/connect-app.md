1. Install the SmartSpin2k Companion App from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smartspin2k-companion-app/id6477836948) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smartspin2k.app).
2. Open the Companion App. On first launch, **Guided Setup** opens. Follow it to the end, or tap **Exit Setup** at the top right to continue with this page.
3. On the **Find Your SmartSpin2k** screen, tap **SCAN**.
4. Tap **CONNECT** next to your SmartSpin2k.
5. The **Device** screen opens. The header shows the device name and firmware version.
