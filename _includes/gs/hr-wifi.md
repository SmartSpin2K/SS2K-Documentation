### Heart rate monitor

SmartSpin2k can relay your heart rate to your training app. This helps on Apple TV, which allows only a few Bluetooth connections.

1. Put on your heart rate monitor so it starts broadcasting.
2. On **Device**, tap **Settings**, **Bluetooth**, then **Saved HRM**.
3. Tap **SCAN**, tap your monitor in the list, then tap **SAVE**.

### WiFi

WiFi lets SmartSpin2k download firmware updates and gives compatible training apps a faster connection.

1. On **Device**, tap **Settings**, **Network**, then **WiFi network**.
2. Enter the **Network name (SSID)** and **Password** of a 2.4 GHz network.
3. Tap **Save to SmartSpin2k**, then tap **Reboot now** in the **Reboot SmartSpin2k?** dialog.
